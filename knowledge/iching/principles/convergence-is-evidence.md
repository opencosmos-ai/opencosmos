# A word is not spent because someone else used it, and agreement is evidence

**The rule.** No English word is unavailable because a prior translation reached it first. A word is ruled out only when something is wrong with **the word** — it imports what the Chinese lacks, it collides with a lock, it renders the wrong thing. "Legge used it" is not a defect in a word. And where translators working independently converge, that convergence is **support for the reading**, not contamination of it.

**When it fires.** Whenever a rendering is rejected, downgraded, or quietly passed over and the reason on the page is a name rather than a property.

---

## Why this holds

**The damage is invisible one entry at a time, and compounds.** Rejecting the best word because Legge got there costs a little on that line and nothing anywhere else — there is no test that fails, no lock that trips, no reviewer who can see it. Repeat it across 64 hexagram names and 512 line texts and the result is a translation systematically worse than it should be, with the cause spread so thin that no reader could ever reconstruct it. **A rule that penalises agreement is a machine for choosing the second-best word.**

**It also inverts the point of reading four corners at once.** [`method.md`](../method.md) §2 says to use the three old translations *as a set*, because where they converge the reading is well supported and where they split the decision is live. Treating convergence as a reason for suspicion turns the most useful signal in the corpus upside down.

**The genuine concern is about derivation, and derivation is a property of the process.** A rendering is borrowed when the decision was outsourced — the entry says "Legge has X" and stops. It is independent when it came from the graph, the contexts and the commentaries. **You cannot tell the two apart by looking at the word**, which is exactly why the test has to be the reasoning in `glossary/` and never the vocabulary.

**And the locks already demonstrate the right form.** 天 is barred from "Heaven" because "Heaven" imports a deity the Chinese does not have. The lock names the harm, and it would hold if no missionary had ever written the word. Every exclusion should be stated that way: say what is wrong with the word.

## The cases

**澤 was ruled toward *marsh* partly on the ground that "lake is Wilhelm's, and excluded."** That reasoning fused two unrelated rules. Wilhelm–Baynes is not vendored because it is a modern translation in copyright — an admission rule about files, in [`../sources/PROVENANCE.md`](../sources/PROVENANCE.md), which places no word out of reach. *Lake* had to be re-argued on the evidence, and it turns out to survive 澤无水 (47) and 澤上有水 (60) at least as well as *marsh* does. → [method.md](../method.md#2-triangulating-meaning--the-four-corners)

**"Never for a word" stood in `method.md` §2 for a day and would have forbidden *thunder* for 雷.** The inherited rule says never for *phrasing*; compressing it to *a word* made an English term of art unreachable because Legge reached it first. → [glossary/README](../glossary/README.md#what-an-entry-owes)

---

## Where it does not fire

**The locks are not this.** 47 terms are barred from specific Englishes, and every one is barred for what the word does — "Heaven" for 天, "king" for 王. Those hold regardless of who wrote them, and a rendering that reaches for one is a defect. See [`../sources/locks/`](../sources/locks/README.md).

**Nor is the admission rule.** Which editions may live in `sources/` is settled by [`PROVENANCE`](../sources/PROVENANCE.md) on rights and provenance. That is about files, and it is not an opinion about vocabulary.

**And it does not license copying.** Taking a reading without deriving it is still borrowing, and it is worse when the borrowed word is right, because then nothing looks wrong. The obligation is the derivation, not the avoidance.

---

## What it obliges

1. **State what is wrong with the word.** If the only objection is its author, there is no objection.
2. **Record convergence as support.** Where the three agree, say so in the entry and count it in the reading's favour.
3. **Put the derivation in `glossary/`**, so independence is demonstrable from the page rather than asserted.
4. **Re-open anything ruled out on authorship.** An exclusion made for the wrong reason is not made.
