## The judgment

(This Diagram implics) stopping at the back, ‘and not regarding the body; walking into the courtyard but not meeting any one;* no injury ensues. -2,

## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Win Wang says:—Kăn means “to stop;” at the proper time for stopping, then stop; and, at the proper time for acting, then act. Motion and Rest are not without proper periods for their exercise; their path is luminous and bright—Stopping, ‘in the Kăn Diagram, means stopping at the proper poiut; the upper and lower ranks in opposition to each other ([= and =), and not having mutual intercourse; this is what is meant by not regarding self, going into a courtyard and not meeting any one, and no injury resulting.

## 大象傳 — "Chow Kung says"

Chow Kung says :—Two Mountains (== and ==) constitute the Kin Diagram. By it the Model Man reflects upon not retiring from his post of influence.
