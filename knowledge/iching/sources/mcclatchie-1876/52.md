## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Win Wing says:-Kin means "to stop;" at the proper time for stopping, then stop; and, at the proper time for acting, then act. Motion and Rest are not without proper periods for their exercise; their path is luminous and bright—Stopping, in the Kan Diagram, menus mutual intercourse; this is wllat is meant by not regarding self, going into a courtyard and not meeting auy ouc, and no injury resulting.

## 大象傳 — "Chow Kung says"
