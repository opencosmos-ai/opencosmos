## The lines

**First-Six.** Represents a stranger of contracted mind; it is thus that calamity is induced.. Chow Kung says:—A stranger of contracted.mind, implies that the will is at the extreme of weakness, and hence calamity arises.

**Second-Six.** Is, a stranger dwelling at an inn and storing up his goods; he is treated with strict rectitude by the servants, Chow Kung says :—Being treated with strict rectitude: by the servants, implies the final absence of all xesentment.

**Third-Nine.** Repfesents a stranger setting his inn on fire; he thus loses the faithful services of the attendants, and is rendered anxious, Chow Kung says :—A stranger setting fire to his inn? also suffers injury himself. ‘To treat servants as strangers | must necessarily cause loss (to one’s self). »
