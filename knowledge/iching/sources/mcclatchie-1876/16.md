## The judgment

(During the period of the) Yu Diagram, it is advantagcous to appoint Nobility of the second rank, and, ‘to mobilize the army..

Chow Kung says:—Thunder ⟦trigram figure⟧ rushing forth from Earth ⟦trigram figure⟧ constitutes the Yu Diagram. By it the ancient Kings invented music, lauded virtue, and offered. numerous sacrifices to the Supreme Emperor (£ ar) with whom they paired their own ancestors,
