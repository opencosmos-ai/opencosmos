# Hexagram 40 · 解 — McClatchie 1876

> He calls a hexagram a **Diagram**, renders 君子 as **"the Model Man"**, and attributes
> 彖 to "Wăn Wang" and 象 to "Chow Kung". The Chinese faces this on scan pages 219, 221 and is not transcribed:

## The judgment

—— fiz ” 1. During the Keüe “Diagram there is profit in the — South-west; if no way of egress (can be found), then turning back is attended with good luck; if it is necessary | to proceed in any direction, then diligence in the morning is attended with good luck.

## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Win Wang says :—The Keiie Diagram is Danger ⟦trigram figure⟧ and Motion ⟦trigram figure⟧; by motion escaping from danger—In the Keäe period benefit being obtained at the South-west, implies that in progression (the hearts of) the multitude are secured. Returning being lucky, means that the due Medium has been attained. Early diligence being lucky when about to proceed in any direction, implies that advancing is meritorious—Heaven. and Earth being unloosed thunder and rain are produced; and thunder and rain existing, f·uits, plants, and trees, all send forth buds; how great then is the period of the Keäe Diagram! _

## 大象傳 — "Chow Kung says"

Chow Kung says :—Thunder ⟦trigram figure⟧ and Rain ⟦trigram figure⟧ arising, constitute the Keüe Diagram, By it the Model, Man forgives faults and remits offences, “4,

## The lines

**First-Six.** Signifies the absence of danger. Chow Kung says :—In the blending of the Hard and the Soft there should be no danger.. SE eee ee

**Second-Niue.** Signifies catching three foxes in hunting,’ and obtaining the Yellow arrow;? by preserving moral undeflectedness good luck is secured. Chow Kung says:—In the Secoud-Nine (stroke) good luck resulting from the preservation of moral rectitude, implies that the due Medium is attained.

**Third-Six.** Represents a carrier riding in a carriage, ’ thus causing banditti to attack him; even if: moral rectitude he preserved, yet, shame results. Chow Kung says:—A carrier riding in a carriage is most detestable;* this is one’s-self causing plunderers to: approach, and then who else is to blame? ‘7.

**Fourth-Nine.** Signifies cutting off the toes;* friends then approach and immediately confide in you. Chow Kung says:—Cutting off the toes, means (those) not occupying a proper position...

**Fifth-Six.** Signifies the Model Man merely extricating himself (from inferior companionship) and is lucky; this is verified by observing inferior men. - Chow Kung says:—The Model Man being liberated, means that inferior men withdraw from him,

**Sixth-Six.** Represents a Duke discharging an. arrow at an eagle on the top of a high wall; if he catches it, that is by no means disadvantageous. Chow Kung says:—A duke discharging an arrow at an eagle, means putting down rebellion, mrerocmecmenes. 1. Punishing evil persons. 2. Attaining the due Medium, which is straight like an arrow. Yellow is the Middle. or dne Medium colour. See plate, iv. 3. That is, an inferior person in an exalted position. 4. That is, dismissing inferior persons whose place te below.
