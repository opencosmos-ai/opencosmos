## The judgment

a ememmenenneneee gene — 1, The Ta-Chŭh Diagram embraces Benefit and Completion; not living in the family (@. ¢ taking office) is now attended with good luck; benefit is obtained in wading through great streams (i. e. ¢. overcoming difficulties).

## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Wăn Wang says:—Ta-Chŭh is the splendour of Hardness and firmness, sincerity and reality; their virtus is daily renewed—The Hard ascending and reverencing moral worth, (this Diagram) can both stop and establish (things), and is perfectly undeflected—Not living in retirement being now lucky, refers to maintaining the worthy (i. e. by giving them public employment)—Obtaining benefit from wading through great streams implies responding to Heaven (in our actions).

## 大象傳 — "Chow Kung says"

Chow Kung says :—Heaven ⟦trigram figure⟧ in the midst of Mountains constitues the Ta-Chŭh Diagram. By it the Model Man remembers the words and walks in the footsteps of the ancients, and thus cherishes virtue..
