## The judgment

The Kheen Diagram implies Luxuriance,.The Model Man obtains (the advantage of ) it throughout his life.

## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Wăn Wang says:—The Kheen Diagram implies Luxuriance; the Heavenly Taou? sends down its beneficial influence, and splendidly adorns all things, and the Earthly Taou? is lowly and ascends, —The Heavenly Taou puts down the haughty, and confers benefits upon the humble;; the Earthly Taou changes (the position of) the haughty and descends upon the humble; Gods and Demons send calamity upon the haughty, and bestow happiness upon the humble; and the Human ‘Taou® hates the haughty and delights in the humble. The humble are honourable and illustrious, and lowliness which eannot We surpassed, marks the entire life of the Model Man.
