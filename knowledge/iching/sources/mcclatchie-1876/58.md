## The lines

**First-Nine.** Signifies mildness and cheerfulness, and is attended with good luck. Chow Kung says :—The good luck attending mildness. and cheerfulness, is that, action is without hesitation.

**Sccond-Nine.** Signifies inspiring confidence by cheerfulness; is lucky, and no regret follows. Chow Kung says :—Good luck attending upon cheerfulness and inspiring confidence, implies the inclination to inspire trust.

**Third-Six.** (Signifies) approaching in order to please (by flattery), and is unlucky. Chow Kung says :—Approaching in order to please is unlucky, because the position occupied is an improper one.’

**Fourth-Nine.** Is, deliberating with a view to please; tranquillity i is not attained by this; being firm in reject-. ing evil brings rejoicing. Chow Kung says :—The rejoicing of the Fourth-Nine stroke, implies the possession of happiness.

**Fifth-Nine.** Is, placing confidence in what is destructive; danger ensucs. Chow Kung says :—Putting confidence in what is destructive, implies occupying an undeflected position (and falling from it).

**Sixth-Six.** Is, leading on to gratification (by flattery or otherwise). Chow Kung says :—The leading on to gratification of the Sixth-Six stroke, implies the absence of all fame.
