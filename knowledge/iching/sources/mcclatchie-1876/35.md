## The judgment

The Tsin Diagram (represents) the Nobles of peaceful States using horses bestowed (by the Emperor) in great numbers; (also) three presents received (from the Emperor). in one day.,

## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Win Wang says:—"Tsin" means progression_ (It represents) Light ⟦trigram figure⟧ coming forth above the Earth ⟦trigram figure⟧;' submissively approaching to great light; the Soft progressing and ascending; thus it is that the Nobles of States at peace use the numerous horses presented, and that three presents are received in one day.

## 大象傳 — "Chow Kung says"

Chow Kung says:---Light coming out above the Earth constitutes the Tsin Diagram. By it the Model Man renders his own luminous virtue resplendent.
