Wan Wang says:-—The Chin Diagram implies Luxuriance.—Thunder coming and causing anxiety, implies.that apprehcusiow (of: doing evil) leads to happiness. Joyful speech causing laughter, implies that after the anxiety (Has passed away) means are devised (to accomplish one's purpose)—Thunder agitating to a distance of one hundred le, means, excitiug the distant and terrifying the near. (Not dropping the sacrificial ladle and wine through fright, means that) even in departing‘ the ancestral temples, and the gods of the land and grain may be retained hy avother (Emperor) acting as the Lord of Sacrifices.’.
