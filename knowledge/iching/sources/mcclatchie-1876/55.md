## The judgment

Huy iP. 1, The Fung Diagram implies Luxuriance. Tho King who reaches this point has no cause for regret; he should resemble the meridian sun..

## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Win Wang says :—Fung implies greatiiess; intellicence and_action, and hence, affluence. The King reaching this poiut, means that he should esteem what is great; not experiencing regret, he ought to resemble the meridian sun, means that he ought to eulighten the world (i. e. the Empire). —When the Sun comes to the meridian he declines; when the Moon reaches the full she wanes; Heaven and Earth are alternately full aud empty, and | advance and recede with the times,’ how much more then are Men and the Demon-gods (subject to these changes)?*
