## The judgment

In the Kwei Diagram small undertakings are attended with good luck.

## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Wăn Wang says :—Kwei is Fire ([=) in motion and ascending, Moisture (—) in motion and descending; (it represents) two women residing together, but their inclinations not according—(It is) rejoicing and approaching to the light; the Soft progressing and ascending, attaining the duc Medium (fifth throne) and responding to the Hard; thus it is that small undertakings’ are attended with good luck—Heaven and Earth are opposites, but their employments are the same; male aud female are opposites, but their inclinations accord; amongst the myriad of things are opposites, but their employments are of the same kind (e. gr. receiving dew, rain, &c.); How useful, then, is the period of the Kwei Diagram!

## 大象傳 — "Chow Kung says"

Chow Kung says:—Fire above and Water below, form the Kwei Diagram. By it the Model man is the same as (i. ¢_in body), and differs from (%, ¢, in mind) other men.
