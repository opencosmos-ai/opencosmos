## The judgment

The Lin Diagram embraces Origin, Luxuriance, Benefit, and Completion; in the eight month it is | unlucky.;

## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Wan Wang says:—The Hard growing gradually, is ‘the Lin Diagram—Cheerfuluess ⟦trigram figure⟧ with submission (= 5), the Hard attaining the due Medium and respondg (to the Soft)—Abounding luxuriantly unto perfect undeflectedness is the path of Heaven—Being unlucky in the eight month, means that the termination (of all things) is at hand.’

## 大象傳 — "Chow Kung says"

Chow Kung says :—Moisture ⟦trigram figure⟧ with Earth ⟦trigram figure⟧ above it, constitute the Lin Diagram. By it the Model Man educates men's wills to the utmost, and protects the people without limit.
