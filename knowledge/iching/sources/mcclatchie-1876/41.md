## The judgment

In the Sun-Diagram if confidence exists, that is very lucky; no error whatever attaches (in the use of it); if moral rectitude can be preserved, then advantage arises in all directions—What should be used (when: lessening extravagance)? Two measures of grain aro suflicient for sacrificial purposes.'

## 彖傳 — "Wăn Wang says"

Wăn Wang says :—Sun is lessening (the property of) inferiors, to add to (that of) superiors; profit now ascends (to those in authority)—Diminishing (by taxation | for iustance) and yet inspiring confidence is very lucky; and no fault arises; if moral rectitude is preserved then benefit is obtained in every direction. What should be used? Two measures of grain (under straightened circumstances) are suflicient for sacrificial purposes. ‘There is a season when two measures ought to be used, and ‘there is also a scason when the Hard (i. e. more than sufficient) should be lessened, and the Soft (/. e. insufficient) added to. Diminishing and adding to, fulness aud emptiness, all go with the times,”

**First-Ninc.** Represents occupations finished, and quickly going forward (to pay taxes); no fault arises; (it also signifies) deliberating and deducting (the amount). Chew Kung says:—(Daily) occupations ended and quickly going forward, implies that the inclinations of the superior ranks accord with those of the inferior ranks, |

**Second-Nine.** Signifies profit arising from the preservation of moral rectitude; to depart from it is unlucky; not diminishing (one’s own possessions) is advantageous (to superiors). Chow Kung says :—In the Second-Nine (stroke) profit resulting from the preservation of moral rectitude, implica that the inclinations accord with the due Medium,

**Third-Six.** Signifies three persons walking together, in which case, one should be sent away;? one walking by himself can obtain a companion,. Chow Kung says :—One person may succeed (in any undertaking), but when there are three, then doubt arises,.
