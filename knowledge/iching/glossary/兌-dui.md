# 兌 — open: what kind of water is 澤

*Rewritten 2026-09-12. The first version ruled out **lake** on the ground that it is
Wilhelm's, which is not a reason — see [[convergence-is-evidence]]. Every candidate below is
argued on what it does to the fifteen images, and the field is open.*

## What the text says 澤 is

All fifteen 大象傳, which is the whole evidence:

| | |
|---|---|
| 10 履 上天下澤 · 19 臨 澤上有地 · 38 睽 上火下澤 · 31 咸 山上有澤 · 41 損 山下有澤 | 澤 as a **tier**, stacked above or below another |
| 17 隨 澤中有雷 · 49 革 澤中有火 | something is **inside** it |
| 28 大過 澤滅木 | it **drowns** what is in it |
| 43 夬 澤上于天 · 45 萃 澤上于地 | it **rises** |
| 54 歸妹 澤上有雷 · 60 節 澤上有水 · 61 中孚 澤上有風 | something sits **on** it |
| 58 兌 麗澤 | two of them **joined** |
| **47 困 澤无水** | **it can be empty** |

**Two of these constrain the English hard.** 澤无水 (47) — "澤 without water" — is incoherent
if 澤 *is* the water, so 澤 is the hollow that holds it. And 澤上有水 (60, 節, limits) puts water
*above* 澤: the basin has a capacity, which is what the hexagram is about. Whatever English is
chosen has to survive being both empty and distinct from its contents.

**And it cannot be "water",** because 坎 is water. The pair is the contrast: 坎 the element you
fall into, 兌 the gathered body that holds.

## The candidates

| | 47 澤无水 | 60 澤上有水 | 31 山上有澤 | 58 麗澤 | register |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **lake** | the lake without water — a dry bed, and a good image for 困 | water above the lake — strains, a lake *is* water | a lake on the mountain — clean | joined lakes — clean | neutral, physical |
| **marsh** | natural — wetlands dry out | fine | upland bog — odd against 咸's exchange | fine | bog, stagnation, mosquitoes |
| **pool** | fine | fine | fine | fine | small; 澤滅木 cannot drown a tree |
| **basin** | exact | exact | fine | odd | geological, cold |
| **moisture** (McClatchie, 12×) | no — moisture cannot lack water | no | *moisture on the mountain* is lovely | no | loses the tier entirely |

**McClatchie's *Moisture* is not a mistake and deserves its hearing.** 澤's range genuinely
includes moisture, lustre, and beneficence — 恩澤, kindness — and on a mountain (31 咸, 41 損)
*moisture* reads as dew and seepage where *lake* reads as a crater. But it cannot be a tier that
something sits inside (49 澤中有火) or that can be empty (47), and the 大象傳 need a tier.

## Where this stands

**`lake` and `marsh` are both live, and `lake` is the stronger of the two on the evidence** —
it survives thirteen of the fifteen cleanly, it pairs with 山 in the way 咸 and 損 need, and its
one real strain (60) is a strain in the Chinese too. *Marsh* is the better fit for 47 and the
worse fit for the trigram's own sense: 說卦 ch 7 glosses 兌 **說** (= 悅), **delight**, and ch 11
gives 少女 and 口舌 — the youngest daughter, the mouth. 兌 is the pleasant trigram, and *marsh*
is a faintly unpleasant English word.

**That the popular English is already *lake* is not an argument either way.** It is not a reason
to take it and it is not a reason to avoid it.

## What is ruled out, and why

Nothing, yet — `forbidden:` is empty deliberately. *The Joyous* would render the **action** where
the 大象傳 stack **images**, which is the same objection that rules out *the Receptive* for 坤 and
*the Abysmal* for 坎; it is not listed until the rendering is settled, because a forbidden list
is a ruling and there is no ruling here.
