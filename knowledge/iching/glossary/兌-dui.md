# 兌 — open: 澤 is not a lake, and "marsh" may be too small

**澤 is low ground where water stands** — marsh, wetland, pool — and by extension **moisture,
lustre, and beneficence**, the shine water leaves on what it touches. The word covers a range
that no single English word covers, and the three available Englishes each take a different part
of it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Legge 1882** | *marsh* — the terrain |
| **McClatchie 1876** | ***Moisture***, 12 times — "Moisture above Mountains forms the Han Diagram" (31 咸) |
| **Wilhelm–Baynes** | *the lake* — **excluded**: a modern translation, and it is where popular English got "lake" |

McClatchie's choice is the interesting one and it is not a mistake. 澤 in 咸 (31, 山上有澤) and
損 (41, 山下有澤) sits against a mountain, and *moisture on a mountain* reads as dew and seepage
where *a lake on a mountain* reads as a crater lake — a much stranger image than the Chinese
asks for. He is the only one of the three whose Image sections are vendored in English.

## The live question

**`marsh` is what 澤 is; `moisture` is what 澤 does.** The 大象傳 stack tiers physically — 澤上有
風 (61), 澤滅木 (28), 澤中有火 (49) — and a tier wants a terrain word, which argues for *marsh*.
But 兌's own sense in 說卦 ch 7 is 說 (= 悅), **delight**, and ch 11 gives 少女 and 口舌, the
youngest daughter and the mouth; the trigram is the pleasant one. *Marsh* in English carries
bog, stagnation and mosquitoes — it is a faintly unpleasant word for the trigram of pleasure.
*Moisture* keeps the gleam and loses the tier.

**Recommendation: `marsh`.** It is the terrain the 大象傳 stacks, it pairs correctly with 山, and
under the clarified rule in [`method.md`](../method.md) §2 taking it is not borrowing from Legge
— it is the English word for the thing. *Lake* is more comfortable and less true, and it arrives
from the one translation this project excludes. The delight belongs in the notes.

**But the register objection is real**, and if Shalom would rather the trigram of delight not be
called a bog, the honest alternatives are *wetland* (neutral, flat) or McClatchie's *moisture*
(true to the gleam, wrong as a tier). **Blocked on that call.** → [`../WORKLIST.md`](../WORKLIST.md) § A3
